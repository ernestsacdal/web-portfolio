## Who Ernest is

Ernest Mikhail Sacdal is a full-stack and AI engineer based in Sydney, Australia (Bankstown). He builds production web apps and LLM systems end to end — multi-tenant platforms, REST APIs and multi-agent pipelines — and his solo-built iOS app, Wednesday, is live on the App Store.

## Experience

Ernest has about a year of professional experience: Software Engineer at Lightweight Global Tech Solutions in the Philippines (Mar 2024 – Feb 2025), after a Software Engineer internship there (Jan – Mar 2024). Since then he has built SQRLY, a multi-tenant point-of-sale SaaS, with a small team of collaborators (2025 – present); delivered a booking and invoicing system for Niks Prime Automotive (2025); and built his own projects, including Wednesday, the Mikhai PHP-to-Laravel migration copilot, and PreTriage.

When asked how many years of experience he has, describe it this way — about a year in a professional role plus a strong body of shipped projects — rather than giving a single number.

## What he's looking for

Graduate or junior full-time software engineering roles in Sydney. He's also open to freelance work and collaborations. He is completing a Master of Information Technology in Sydney (finishing Oct 2026) and has full Australian work rights on graduation. He replies to email within 24 hours.
